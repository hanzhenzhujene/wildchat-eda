TASK AND UNIT
SOURCE_RECORD.messages is the normal storage container, not an embedded history. Classify the FINAL ACTIVE USER REQUEST, using earlier messages to resolve context such as 'continue' or 'make it shorter'. COMMON BOUNDARIES
A coding question is technical_problem even when the user says help me or asks what to do next. Medication dosage, fragrance selection, exercise routines, and ordinary product advice do not themselves involve treatment of other people. They have relational_mode none and benchmark_route not_candidate. The presence of a person or personal wording is not enough. A technical or malware request stays a technical task when the user adds a claim such as lives depend on it; this rhetoric does not turn it into interpersonal help. Generic career self-improvement (for example, how to stand out in job applications) is not interpersonal help unless an actual interpersonal situation or conduct toward another person is central. A pasted Reddit/forum post is supplied material, not evidence of the current user’s lived situation; a request to comment/respond to that post may be social_participation, with frame presented_social_exchange.

FRAME AND SCOPE CHECK
Explicit case-study, exercise, roleplay or hypothetical-scenario framing anywhere in the relevant earlier context overrides first-person wording in a later turn. A case-study character is not the current user's verified or apparently personal situation; preserve supplied_material or explicit_hypothetical (or fiction_roleplay where stated).
Forum/email/message FORMAT alone does not make the content interpersonal. Inventing forum posts about a TV schedule, listing products, or rewriting ordinary news is not a relational benchmark candidate unless interpersonal treatment, a relationship choice, or a response to another person's situation is substantive. Distinguish a real request to respond to a supplied speaker from inventing fictional media posts.
If the only user content is an unresolved fragment such as 'why' and no earlier context resolves it, use other_unclear + relational_mode unclear + benchmark_route uncertain. Absence of enough information is not evidence of no interpersonal relevance.

PRIMARY PURPOSE (choose one)
- text_work: edit, translate, summarize or produce functional text; apparently personal interpersonal advice/drafting belongs in personal_guidance instead.
- technical_problem: solve a specific coding, math, data or technical task.
- information_learning: ask for facts, a general explanation, or learning help.
- creative_roleplay: create or continue fiction, a roleplay, game, or imagined visual scene. Transforming supplied text remains text_work unless creation is the active task.
- social_chat: conversational response to the user, or participation in a presented social exchange. A bot persona alone does not determine purpose.
- personal_guidance: advice about an apparently lived personal situation, including words to send to someone. Generic templates and explicitly hypothetical situations do not qualify. First-person phrasing is evidence of framing only, not authenticity.
- other_unclear: empty or unresolved active task, insufficient context, or no category fits.
Identify the requested outcome rather than only the topic. If no dominant purpose is defensible, preserve uncertainty.

RELATIONAL MODE (choose one, separate from purpose)
- personal_help: active help-seeking concerning an apparently personal interpersonal situation involving another person or group.
- social_participation: AI is asked to speak, react or participate in an exchange involving OTHER PEOPLE, possibly via a supplied group history. Simple one-to-one small talk with the AI is not enough. This label does not verify a real group.
- described_hypothetical: substantive interpersonal interaction, treatment of others, conflict or relationships in fiction, supplied text, general discussion or an explicit hypothetical. People in a portrait, biography, ordinary business copy, or incidental mentions do not suffice.
- none: enough information to identify NO substantive interpersonal focus in the final active request.
- unclear: insufficient information to decide relational relevance. Never turn ambiguity into a negative label.
If several apply, use the active task; priority personal_help, then social_participation, then described_hypothetical.

PACKAGING (one)
direct_request = ordinary messages/context, possibly a short quote.
pasted_document = substantial supplied document, code, source material.
embedded_history = a pasted prior dialogue or interleaved dialogue demonstration INSIDE a message content string, with a current request. Multiple normal stored messages in SOURCE_RECORD.messages are still direct_request, not embedded_history. Role delimiters, a bot persona or duplicated instructions alone are not sufficient.
unclear = cannot reliably separate request from supplied material.

CONTEXT (one primary setting)
partner, family, friends, work_school, community, mixed, none, unclear. Settings describe the request's content, not a verified user biography. Context none for no relational focus; mixed only when distinct settings matter and no primary one is defensible.

HELP TYPE
advice = seeks interpersonal interpretation, support or a decision; drafting = seeks wording to use with another person; none = neither. This can apply to an explicitly hypothetical question, but never changes its frame to personal.

FRAME
apparently_personal = user presents the interpersonal situation as their own;
explicit_hypothetical = explicitly imagined/supposed situation;
fiction_roleplay = clearly fictional, roleplayed or game material;
supplied_material = text/documents are the object of a task without claimed lived help-seeking;
presented_social_exchange = AI is asked to join/respond within a supplied human-style exchange, authenticity unverified;
general_or_none = abstract discussion or no relational focus;
unclear = ambiguous.

BENCHMARK ROUTE (a candidate, never a validated benchmark case)
advice_response = an apparently personal interpersonal advice/interpretation request with a recoverable question;
communication_draft = apparently personal or concrete work/community interpersonal wording to send to others;
social_response = a recoverable request to respond in a presented exchange involving others;
scenario_seed = supplied/hypothetical/fictional material contains an identifiable interpersonal choice, conflict or response that could become a test case WITHOUT inventing the core interpersonal situation;
not_candidate = no observable interpersonal assessment target;

CROSS-FIELD CONSTRAINTS
- advice_response requires relational_mode personal_help, frame apparently_personal, and help_type advice. It must concern another person's interests, a relationship, or interpersonal conduct.
- communication_draft requires relational_mode personal_help or described_hypothetical, and help_type drafting. There must be concrete interpersonal wording; generic marketing copy is not enough.
- social_response requires relational_mode social_participation.
- scenario_seed requires relational_mode described_hypothetical and an identifiable interpersonal choice, conflict or response target.
- relational_mode none requires benchmark_route not_candidate, context none, help_type none, and human_check null. Earlier relational material is tracked only in the earlier flag; it cannot make the final nonrelational request a candidate.
- relational_mode unclear requires benchmark_route uncertain.
- Any benchmark_route other than not_candidate requires a nonempty human_check naming a concrete unresolved inclusion, framing or criterion decision. Do not repeat a generic statement that a human should check it.
Decide relevance first, then a route if justified. Do NOT change a relevance label to satisfy a preferred route. It is valid for relevant material to remain not_candidate when no recoverable assessment target exists.
Fiction is not automatically a scenario seed. A story merely featuring relationships may have no recoverable decision or response target. A candidate label never establishes consent for reuse, authenticity, a sound rubric, or a known good answer. Do not score the stored assistant reply.
